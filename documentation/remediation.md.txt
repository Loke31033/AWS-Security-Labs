# IAM Security Remediation

## Finding

**IAM-002 — Excessive IAM Privilege**

---

## Issue

The `cloudsec-analyst` identity was intended to perform read-only IAM activities.

A temporary policy was attached to the `CloudSecurity-Analysts` group that granted:

```text
iam:CreateUser


This permission exceeded the intended responsibilities of the analyst role.

Security Risk

Excessive permissions increase the potential impact of compromised credentials.

An identity capable of creating IAM users may become a security risk when combined with other permissions that allow privilege assignment or administrative activity.

Therefore, unnecessary IAM permissions should be removed.

Root Cause

The excessive permission originated from the intentionally attached policy:

LAB-EXCESSIVE-IAM-CreateUser

Policy effect:

Allow

Action:

iam:CreateUser

Resource:

*
Remediation Procedure
Step 1 — Identify the excessive policy

The following policy was identified as the source of the unnecessary permission:

LAB-EXCESSIVE-IAM-CreateUser
Step 2 — Remove the policy

The policy was removed from:

CloudSecurity-Analysts
Step 3 — Verify remaining permissions

The group retained only the intended read-only IAM policy:

CloudSecurityAnalyst-ReadOnly-IAM
Step 4 — Retest

The IAM Policy Simulator was used to test:

iam:CreateUser
Result
DENIED
Before Remediation
cloudsec-analyst
       |
       v
CloudSecurity-Analysts
       |
       +-- IAM Read Only
       |
       +-- CreateUser  <-- Excessive Permission
After Remediation
cloudsec-analyst
       |
       v
CloudSecurity-Analysts
       |
       +-- IAM Read Only
Validation

The remediation was considered successful because:

iam:ListUsers
       |
       v
    ALLOWED

iam:CreateUser
       |
       v
    DENIED

The final configuration matches the intended least-privilege model.

Security Principle

Principle of Least Privilege

Users should receive only the permissions necessary to perform their assigned responsibilities.

Final Status

Remediated
