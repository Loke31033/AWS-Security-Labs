IAM-002 — Excessive IAM privilege

Affected identity:

cloudsec-analyst

Expected access:

Read-only IAM

Actual access:

Can create IAM users

Problem:

The analyst's permissions exceed their business requirement.

Risk

An identity capable of creating IAM users could potentially be abused to establish additional identities or persistence, especially if additional permissions are granted elsewhere.

So the issue is:

Privilege exceeds business requirement → violation of least privilege.
